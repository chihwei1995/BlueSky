Global Climate Co-Creation

Collaboration & Vision for Blue Sky Returns

Blue Sky Returns is a global climate education initiative by JCI Peninsula. We invite chapters, schools, youth groups, and community partners to contribute local stories of climate crisis and local hope, and turn them into a shared educational E-book.

Project Brief

What we are building

We are co-creating a global environmental E-book that helps the next generation understand both the climate crisis already happening around us and the local solutions that are giving us hope.

Why it matters

By connecting real stories from cities and regions, climate change becomes more local, visible, and actionable for schools, youth groups, and community partners.

Participation Journey

Level 1

Co-Promote

Help share the project locally and invite more youth and organizations to participate.

Level 2

Co-Create Local Data

Upload local Hope and Crisis materials, including images or short videos and brief descriptions from your city or region.

Level 3

Educational Outreach

After the E-book is completed, help share it with schools, youth groups, and educational partners in your city.

Level 4

Deep Collaboration

Explore deeper partnership through online events, regional campaigns, educational collaboration, and cross-city exchange.
