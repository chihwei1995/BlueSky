Flexible Climate Participation

自定義 7 日綠色提案

我們不直接規定參與者每天要做什麼，而是提供五大綠色維度，讓大家在 7 天內自由組合、重複、交叉上傳，用更真實也更可持續的方式練習低碳生活。

Concept / 概念

This is not a rigid task list. It is an open framework.

Not prescriptive

Participants start from their own pace and context.

Photo-first participation

One photo can capture one conscious choice.

🍀 Any order 📷 Repeat dimensions 🧩 Mix and overlap 🌍 Rooted in real life

The Green Dimensions / 五大維度

Any photo that fits one dimension can count toward the 7-day proposal.

Green Diet

Plant-forward food choices

Examples: meat-free meals, home-cooked vegetables, reducing food waste.

Low-carbon Mobility

Move with less carbon

Examples: walking, cycling, buses, metro, or choosing not to drive.

Waste Reduction

Reduce and reuse

Examples: reusable containers, repairs, recycling, refusing single-use plastic.

Energy Saving

Save energy and trim digital waste

Examples: natural light, unplugging devices, deleting expired emails.

City / Nature Connection

Reconnect urban life with nature

Examples: green infrastructure, community parks, indoor plants, Green Hunt stops.

7-Day Flow / 7 天參與節奏

A light participation rhythm with lots of creative room.

Day 1–7: capture what feels real

  • Find one moment each day that fits one of the five dimensions.
  • If a day gets busy, you can make it up the next day.
  • The same dimension can appear multiple times.

Upload with a short caption

  • Each photo can include one short line explaining what happened.
  • You can label the matching dimensions.
  • You can also share the reason or challenge behind it.

Why This Works / 為什麼這樣設計

An open format is easier to adopt across different cities, ages, and lifestyles.

Lower pressure, higher participation

People are less likely to quit after missing one day.

Better for storytelling

The five dimensions naturally become content categories for posts, exhibits, or an e-book.
